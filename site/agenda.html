Fireside chat

Governing growth in an age of technology and risk

Duration30 minFormatListen, then audience Q&ABest forBoards, investors and policymakers

AI is projected to add US$15 trillion to global GDP by 2030, including US$1 trillion across ASEAN. The same growth brings power-hungry infrastructure, workforce disruption and algorithmic bias, and for financiers these unpriced risks are a reason to hesitate.

In this one-to-one conversation, a senior leader shares how they weigh AI's promise against its governance and stewardship risks: who should set the guardrails, how to keep the transition just for workers and communities, and what clarity regulators, developers and investors need to commit for the long term.

Why it matters: Most AI governance debates stay abstract. This conversation is about the decisions a senior leader actually faces.

You will leave with

  • A leader's view of where AI governance should sit: government, boards or markets
  • How just-transition thinking applies to AI-driven job change
  • The signals investors need before AI and sustainability capital moves together

Opening plenary

AI: the missing piece to the energy transition?

Duration50 minFormatPanel with audience Q&ABest forEnergy, infrastructure, real estate and climate tech

AI can optimise grids, forecast renewable output and cut energy waste across industry. It is also driving a surge in data centre demand that is outpacing clean power in parts of Southeast Asia.

This plenary brings together data centre operators, utilities, climate tech founders and policymakers to test the claim that AI will help rather than hinder the energy transition. Panellists examine energy and water efficiency in data centres, the resource risks of rapid build-out, and the climate tech applications that are ready to scale.

Why it matters: Data centre growth is now a grid and water planning issue across Southeast Asia. This is where operators, utilities and innovators meet on it.

You will leave with

  • Where AI is already saving more energy than it uses, and where it is not
  • The efficiency and siting standards shaping data centre growth in Asia
  • Climate tech use cases ready for deployment, not just pilots

Roundtable

Bridging silos: AI adoption in the real economy

Duration75 minFormatHands-on, facilitated tablesBest forPractitioners from every sector

An interactive, cross-sector deep dive. Delegates from different industries sit together to work through the concerns, challenges and pain points of integrating AI into operations, the emerging risks to sustainable adoption, and where collaboration can lower risk and raise returns.

Delegates join one of four facilitated tables and report back to the room. This is the session built for participation: bring your organisation's real questions.

Why it matters: The most valuable 75 minutes for delegates who want peer benchmarks and new collaborators rather than more presentations.

Sustainable supply chains and operationsHow companies use AI to reduce waste, improve procurement and optimise logistics.Sustainable AI infrastructureData centre efficiency, compute optimisation, water and power demand, and responsible model design.AI for nature and resilienceBiodiversity monitoring, climate risk prediction, adaptation and disaster preparedness.Investment and scalingWhat investors need to see to back climate and sustainability tech built on AI.

You will leave with

  • Peer benchmarks on AI adoption from other sectors
  • A shortlist of collaboration opportunities across your table
  • Direct conversations with investors, operators and policymakers

Plenary debate

Plenary debate or partner-led special presentation

Duration45 minFormatLive vote before and afterBest forEveryone; bring your view

The closing plenary puts one of the hardest trade-offs in AI and sustainability to a vote. Two teams argue a motion, the audience votes before and after, and the swing decides the winner.

The motion and format will be announced closer to the summit. If this slot is taken up as a partner-led presentation, it will focus on a deployable AI-for-sustainability solution.

Why it matters: A format that forces clear positions and shows where the room really stands.

You will leave with

  • The strongest case on each side of a live policy question
  • A real-time read of where the room stands
